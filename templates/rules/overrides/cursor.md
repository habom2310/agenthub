## Cursor Defaults & Overrides

- **File Editing & Formatting**:
  Rely on Cursor Composer and fast inline diffs. Maintain indentation, formatting, and structural conventions of existing files without rewriting untouched regions.
- **Tool Discipline**:
  Adhere to the CodeGraph / ripwire / ripgrep moment table before making edits or refactoring.
- **PR Review with Copilot**:
  When creating a PR, run the standard Copilot CLI review round. Attribute PR review and resolution comments to **Cursor**.
- **Context Management**:
  Keep `.cursorrules` and rules concise to preserve prompt context in Composer.
